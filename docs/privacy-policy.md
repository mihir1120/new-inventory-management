# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 4, 2026  
**App name:** Ashokvatika  
**Developer / Publisher:** Ashokvatika  
**Contact email:** `support@ashokvatika.com`  

This Privacy Policy explains how Ashokvatika ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Ashokvatika mobile application (the "App"). This policy is intended to support Google Play requirements, including the Google Play User Data policy and Data safety disclosures.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ashokvatika Android application and related services made available through the App.

## 2. Information We Access or Collect

Depending on how the App is used, we may access or collect the following categories of information:

- **App account and sign-in information:** limited administrator login inputs used to access protected areas of the App.
- **Inventory and business content:** product names, descriptions, stock details, pricing, media references, and related business records entered into the App.
- **Customer and transaction details:** customer names, phone numbers, customer IDs, transaction codes, and saved deal information entered by authorized users.
- **Access request records:** customer approval or access request details managed within the App.
- **Photos, videos, and files selected by the user:** media chosen from device storage, gallery providers, or document providers for product and catalog use.
- **Camera-captured images:** images taken in the App when an authorized user chooses to capture media.
- **Technical and usage data processed by service providers:** limited device, network, diagnostic, experiment, and analytics-related information that may be processed by Firebase or Android system services to provide backend functionality, analytics, and controlled feature testing.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information processed through the App to:

- provide product, inventory, OTC, cart, catalog, and saved-deal functionality;
- store and sync inventory and approval data across authorized app sessions;
- let authorized users add, edit, view, and share product media;
- support internal business workflows, customer servicing, and transaction tracking;
- measure product behavior and compare feature variants through controlled experimentation and analytics;
- secure and maintain the App and associated backend services;
- comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms and Conditions, and resolve abuse or security issues.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell personal or sensitive user data.

We may share or process data in the following limited ways:

- **Firebase / Google services:** the App uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, and Analytics, to support backend authentication, data storage/sync, feature configuration, and experiment measurement.
- **User-initiated sharing:** when a user selects one or more images and uses the App’s share features, the selected media is shared with the destination chosen by the user, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Messages, or another supported application.
- **Service providers and legal compliance:** data may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our rights, prevent fraud, or protect users, the App, or the public.

## 5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect data handled by the App. These measures include:

- use of HTTPS/TLS-backed Google services where applicable;
- restricted in-app admin access flow;
- disabled Android backup for app data in release builds;
- disabled cleartext traffic in release configuration;
- release-build code shrinking and security-focused configuration.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, app functionality, dispute resolution, compliance, and security purposes.

- Inventory, catalog, media references, saved deals, and access request records may remain stored until removed by an authorized administrator or until no longer needed.
- Some local device data, caches, and temporary files may remain on the device until removed by the system, deleted by the user, or overwritten by the App.
- Shared media sent to third-party apps is thereafter governed by those third parties’ policies.

## 7. User Choices and Controls

Users and administrators may be able to:

- choose whether to capture or upload media;
- remove selected images before sharing them;
- edit or delete business records from within the App, where the feature is available;
- contact us to request correction or deletion of backend data, subject to legal and operational requirements.

To make a privacy request, contact: `support@ashokvatika.com`

## 8. Permissions and Device Access

The App may request or use access to:

- **Internet access** to communicate with backend services;
- **camera** when the user captures product images;
- **photos, media, files, or document access** when the user chooses images or videos for gallery, brochure, or catalog workflows.

The App is intended to access only the media or files needed for the user-facing functionality selected by the user.

## 9. Children

The App is intended for business and operational use and is not directed to children.

## 10. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party infrastructure and operating-system integrations, including Google Firebase, Android document providers, and user-selected share destinations. Their handling of data is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

## 11. International Processing

Depending on the configuration of our service providers, data may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where the user is located.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means the updated policy will apply to future use of the App.
